Privacy Policy for Alarvia

Effective Date: May 26, 2026

Welcome to Alarvia, a medicine reminder and caregiver support application provided by ZeroDevInc (“ZeroDevInc”, “we”, “our”, or “us”).

This Privacy Policy explains how Alarvia collects, uses, stores, and protects your information when you use the application (“Service”).

By using Alarvia, you agree to the practices described in this Privacy Policy.

1. Information We Collect

A. Account Information

When you create an account, we may collect:

  • Name
  • Email address
  • Authentication identifiers
  • Account preferences

B. Medicine and Schedule Data

To provide reminder functionality, we may collect:

  • Medicine names
  • Dosage schedules
  • Reminder times
  • Medicine notes entered by users
  • Medicine intake history
  • Caregiver-related schedule information

This information is used solely to provide app functionality.

C. Device and Technical Information

We may automatically collect:

  • Device type and model
  • Operating system version
  • App version
  • Notification token (FCM token)
  • Crash reports and diagnostics
  • Language and regional settings
  • Time zone information

D. Notification and Alarm Data

To deliver reminders and caregiver alerts, Alarvia may process:

  • Alarm schedules
  • Notification settings
  • Reminder status
  • Caregiver notification preferences

E. Subscription and Payment Information

If you purchase premium features:

  • Payments are processed by Google Play or Apple App Store
  • We do not directly store full payment card information
  • Subscription status and purchase metadata may be stored for account management

2. How We Use Information

We use collected information to:

  • Provide medicine reminder functionality
  • Deliver alarms and notifications
  • Synchronize schedules across devices
  • Enable caregiver support features
  • Improve app reliability and performance
  • Respond to support requests
  • Prevent abuse and unauthorized access
  • Manage subscriptions and premium access

3. Caregiver and Shared Access Features

If caregiver features are enabled:

  • Authorized caregivers may access medicine schedules and reminder information associated with linked accounts
  • Users control caregiver permissions and may revoke access at any time

Users are responsible for managing caregiver access appropriately.

4. Notifications and Permissions

Alarvia may request permissions including:

  • Notifications
  • Exact alarms
  • Foreground services
  • Vibration
  • Audio access for alarms and TTS
  • Internet access

These permissions are used solely for core app functionality.

Disabling certain permissions may limit reminder reliability and app features.

5. Data Storage and Security

We implement reasonable technical and organizational measures to help protect user data.

However, no method of electronic storage or internet transmission is completely secure. While we strive to protect information, we cannot guarantee absolute security.

6. Data Retention

We retain information only as long as necessary to:

  • Provide the Service
  • Maintain account functionality
  • Comply with legal obligations
  • Resolve disputes
  • Enforce agreements

Users may request deletion of their account and associated data where applicable.

7. Third-Party Services

Alarvia may use third-party services including:

  • Firebase Cloud Messaging (FCM)
  • Cloud hosting providers
  • Analytics and crash reporting tools
  • Subscription billing platforms
  • Text-to-speech services

These services may process limited technical data necessary for app functionality.

Their handling of information is governed by their own privacy policies.

8. Data Sharing

We do not sell personal data.

We may share limited information:

  • With authorized caregivers linked by the user

9. Children's Privacy

Alarvia may be used under the supervision of parents, guardians, or caregivers.

We do not knowingly collect personal information from children in violation of applicable laws.

Parents or guardians may contact us regarding data concerns involving dependent users.

10. International Data Processing

Depending on infrastructure providers, information may be processed or stored in countries outside your jurisdiction.

By using the Service, you consent to such processing where permitted by law.
